# reviews/

A completed review of one specific piece of Platform (or Core) work.
This folder holds **instances** of a review — it applies the existing
`docs/standards/REVIEW_STANDARD.md` checklist to one deliverable; that
document remains the canonical checklist and is not restated here
(Constitution Article 7/11 reuse-first rule).

## Naming

`RVW-XXXX.md`, sequential, zero-padded to 4 digits.

## Template

See `TEMPLATE.md` in this folder.

## Related

- `docs/standards/REVIEW_STANDARD.md` — the checklist every review
  here applies.
