## Executive Summary

Chapter 37 closes the domain block with the **compliance framework** — how GoldBot
verifies that it actually follows its own Constitution. Compliance ties the whole
document together: the Constitution and the standards are the measure, review and
acceptance are the gates, and the audit trail is the evidence. This chapter states
how conformance is established and kept, continuously.

## 1. Compliance Statement

Compliance is the discipline of **verifying that GoldBot follows its own rules**. A
constitution that is not checked is only an aspiration; the compliance framework
makes conformance demonstrable.

## 2. The Constitution as the Standard

The Constitution — together with the standards and policies it references — is the
measure against which work is judged. Compliance is conformance to these documents,
not to individual preference, so the standard is shared and stable across every work
package.

## 3. Review and Acceptance Gates

Conformance is enforced at review and acceptance (Chapters 13, 14). A change passes
only when it meets the standards, its automated checks succeed, its boundaries are
intact, and its decisions are recorded. A gate that is not met blocks the change
until it is resolved.

## 4. The Audit Trail as Evidence

The audit trail — decisions, reviews, changelog, and acceptance records — is the
evidence of compliance. Because consequential work is recorded, conformance can be
demonstrated after the fact rather than merely claimed (Chapter 15).

## 5. Single Source of Truth

Compliance depends on there being one authoritative source per domain (Chapters 10,
16). Conformance is checked against that single source; duplicated or drifting rules
would make compliance ambiguous, so the compose-and-link discipline is itself a
compliance requirement.

## 6. Standards and Policy Conformance

Work conforms to the operative standards and policies — for code, tests, reviews,
documentation, releases, and versions — which the Constitution references rather
than restates. Compliance is measured against those operative sources, keeping the
detail in one place.

## 7. Compliance and Safety

The highest compliance obligation is Trading Safety. A change that would bypass the
Risk Manager, let the advisory intelligence act, introduce a second entry point, or
place trading logic in the gateway is non-compliant regardless of its other merits,
and the safety gate cannot be waived (Chapters 13, 36).

## 8. Continuous Compliance

Compliance is maintained continuously, not audited once. The gates apply to every
work package alike, known gaps are tracked and retired through the technical-debt
workflow, and lessons are recorded — so conformance does not erode as the ecosystem
grows.
