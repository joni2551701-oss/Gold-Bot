## Executive Summary

Chapter 40 is the **final chapter** of the GoldBot Constitution v1.0 edition. It
states the provisions that make the whole document operative: how it is ratified and
enters into force, how it relates to the in-force Articles-based constitution, how
precedence and severability work, and the affirmation that its safety guarantees are

## 1. Purpose of the Final Provisions

The final provisions bind the edition together into an operative whole. They state
how the Constitution takes effect, how it fits alongside existing governance, and how
it endures — the closing terms that turn forty chapters into one instrument.

## 2. Ratification and Entry into Force

The chaptered edition is adopted **by Director approval**. Each chapter enters the
edition upon its approval (moving through the recorded Draft → Review → Approved →
Completed states), and Version 1.0 is complete when the full set of chapters,
including this one, is approved. Entry into force follows the same governed
authorization as any consequential change (Chapters 11, 15).

## 3. Relationship to the Operative Constitution

The chaptered edition **extends and consolidates** the in-force Articles-based
constitution ([`CONSTITUTION.md`](../CONSTITUTION.md),
[`ARTICLES.md`](../ARTICLES.md), [`AMENDMENTS.md`](../AMENDMENTS.md)); it does not
silently override it. Consistent with Chapter 10, the precise final relationship —
whether the chaptered edition supersedes the Articles edition or formally wraps it —
is determined by a **recorded Director ruling** accompanying the edition's completion.
Until that ruling, where a rule already exists in the Articles or an operative policy,
that source remains operative and the chaptered edition states its intent and points
to it.

## 4. Precedence and Consistency

Within the chaptered edition, the chapters are consistent by construction: reserved
terms carry one meaning (Chapter 07), each domain has a single source of truth
(Chapters 10, 16), and no chapter contradicts an approved chapter or a Director
ruling. Should an inconsistency ever be found, it is resolved by amendment
(Chapter 38), not by silent reinterpretation.

## 5. Severability

If any single provision is found invalid, unworkable, or in need of change, that
provision is amended or set aside under the amendment process **without invalidating
the rest** of the Constitution. The document is designed so that a defect in one
chapter does not bring down the whole; the remaining provisions continue in force.

## 6. Completeness of Version 1.0

Version 1.0 comprises the foundational block (Chapters 01–07), the governance block
(08–17), the architecture block (18–27), the domain block (28–37), and the closing
block (38–40). Upon approval of this chapter, the edition is **complete** as a
coherent whole; further growth proceeds by extension and amendment (Chapters 38, 39),
not by reopening the edition.

## 7. Final Safety Affirmation

The Constitution closes by reaffirming its highest commitment: **Trading Safety is
permanent.** Across every version, provider, surface, and future work package, the
Risk Manager is never bypassed, and the advisory intelligence never approves,
rejects, sizes, sends, or executes a trade. No amendment, roadmap item, or provision
may weaken this. The user's structural protection is non-negotiable and
non-expiring.

## 8. Continuation

With Version 1.0 complete, GoldBot continues under this Constitution: the Core stays
stable, the ecosystem grows on top of the gateway as governed work packages, and the
document is extended routinely and amended rarely. The Constitution is written once,
carefully, so that the years ahead add to it rather than rewrite it.
