## Executive Summary

Chapter 39 states the **roadmap and evolution model** — where GoldBot goes after its
Core is complete, and the single rule by which it gets there. With the Core
Infrastructure finished, the roadmap is a sequence of surfaces and capabilities added
on top of the gateway, each as a governed work package. This chapter states the
baseline, the forward work, and the evolution principles that keep growth safe.

## 1. Roadmap Statement

The roadmap describes GoldBot's **planned evolution**: the ordered work by which the
system grows on top of a completed Core. It is a plan for addition, not for
rewriting, consistent with the vision and long-term goals (Chapters 01, 05).

## 2. The Core-Complete Baseline

The roadmap begins from a **Core-complete baseline** — the finished Core
Infrastructure (Memory, Persistent Memory, Events, Replay, Snapshots, Gateway, and
their supporting foundations), reached at the Core-complete milestone. From this
baseline, further work is built on top of the gateway rather than into the Core.

## 3. Phase 2 Work Packages

The forward work is organized into work packages, each a governed surface or
capability on top of the gateway: a **Chart** surface rendering Core data and replay;
**Personal AI** advisory intelligence; **Platform** clients on one shared model; and
**Media** surfaces built on chart and broadcast. Each work package is delivered under
the full cadence — architecture, documentation, implementation, testing, review, and
acceptance — and consumes the Core only through the gateway.

## 4. The Growth Model

Growth follows one repeatable model (Chapter 05): a new capability enters as a work
package that registers as a governed service or surface, passes design and
documentation before code, and is reviewed before merge. The Core is not modified to
accommodate a surface; surfaces adapt to the Core. This keeps the cost of the next
addition low and the risk to the foundation bounded.

## 5. Milestones

The roadmap advances by milestones, each a set of governed work packages with their
own reviews and acceptance records: Core complete; then surface foundations (chart
and intelligence); then platform maturity across clients; then ecosystem maturity as
media and further surfaces build on existing ones. No milestone is reached by
weakening a boundary.

## 6. Evolution Principles

Evolution obeys the future-proof principles (Chapter 01): a stable Core with evolving
edges; one gateway forever; provider- and transport-agnostic design; versioned
compatibility so parts evolve at different speeds; and amend rarely, extend
routinely. These principles are the tests every roadmap item must pass.

## 7. Roadmap and Safety

No roadmap item may weaken Trading Safety. Every future surface, provider, or
capability preserves the guarantees that the Risk Manager is never bypassed and that
the advisory intelligence never executes (Chapters 07, 36). Safety is a constant of
the roadmap, not a variable.

## 8. The Living Roadmap

The roadmap is a living plan: the Director sets and re-orders it (Chapter 11), and it
grows as work packages are added and completed, each closing with recorded lessons
and cross-project notes. The direction may adjust; the growth model and the safety
guarantees behind it do not.
