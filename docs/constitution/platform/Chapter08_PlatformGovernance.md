## Executive Summary

Chapter 08 opens the Platform governance block by stating **how the Platform layer is
governed**. Platform governance operates under — and never above — the GoldBot Constitution:
it directs how surfaces are designed, reviewed, and delivered, while inheriting every Core
protection unchanged. This chapter states the governing convictions that the later platform
governance chapters make concrete.

## 1. Platform Governance Statement

The Platform layer is governed to deliver surfaces that are **consistent, thin, and safe**,
under the GoldBot Constitution. Platform governance sets how surface work is directed,
reviewed, and accepted; it never changes Core governance or the safety guarantees.

## 2. Purpose

Platform governance exists to keep surface work aligned with the platform vision and mission
and with the GoldBot Constitution: consistency across surfaces, gateway-only access, no
trading logic, and preserved safety. It is protection and alignment expressed as process.

## 3. Principles

- **Subordination.** The GoldBot Constitution governs; platform governance operates within it.
- **Consistency first.** Surface work preserves one coherent, shared model.
- **Review before effect.** Platform changes are reviewed before they take effect.
- **Single source of truth.** Platform governance defers to the GoldBot Constitution and the
  operative platform policies rather than duplicating them.
- **Reuse before creation.** Existing surfaces and the shared model are reused before new ones
  are built.

## 4. Authority Under the GoldBot Constitution

All platform authority is **derived and bounded**. No platform role or process may override
the GoldBot Constitution, introduce a second entry point to the Core, place trading logic in a
surface, or weaken a safety guarantee. Where platform governance and Core governance meet, the
GoldBot Constitution prevails.

## 5. Separation of Platform Roles

Platform governance separates the roles that direct product work, execute it, and review it
(Chapters 11–13), so no single role both authors and unilaterally approves a consequential
surface change. This separation operates beneath the GoldBot Director's overall authority.

## 6. No Silent Decisions

A consequential platform choice that cannot be resolved from the brief is escalated for a
ruling — to the Product Director, and where it touches Core or safety, to the GoldBot Director
— never made quietly. Silence is not consent (GoldBot Constitution, Decision Process).

## 7. Governance and Safety

Platform governance inherits the non-amendable safety guarantees (DR-015) and exists in part
to protect them at the surface: no platform process may be used to bypass the risk controls or
grant a surface a route to act. Safety is inherited whole and never weakened.

## 8. Long-Term Governance

Over the long horizon, platform governance stays **light and stable**: roles endure, the
process for delivering a surface stays recognizable, and platform governing documents are
amended rarely and extended routinely, always under the GoldBot Constitution.
