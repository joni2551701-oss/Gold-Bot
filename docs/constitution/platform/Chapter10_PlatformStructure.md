## Executive Summary

Chapter 10 documents the **structure of the Platform Constitution itself** — how it is
organized, how its chapters are shaped, and how it relates to the GoldBot Constitution. A
structure that mirrors the GoldBot Constitution keeps the two editions consistent and easy to
navigate together.

## 1. Purpose of the Structure

The Platform Constitution is structured to be **extended routinely and amended rarely**, and
to mirror the GoldBot Constitution's organization so the two read as one coherent body of
governance.

## 2. Relationship to the GoldBot Constitution

The Platform Constitution is a **subordinate edition** (DR-013): the GoldBot Constitution v1.0
is the primary governing document, and the Platform Constitution governs the Platform layer
beneath it. Where a matter is Core or safety, the GoldBot Constitution governs; the Platform
Constitution states platform-scoped rules and defers upward for the rest.

## 3. Blocks and Chapters

The Platform Constitution is organized into blocks that parallel the GoldBot Constitution:

- **Foundation (01–07):** Vision, Mission, Values, Core Principles, Long-Term Goals, Scope,
  Terminology — platform-scoped.
- **Governance (08–17):** Platform Governance, Product Philosophy, Platform Structure, Product
  Director, Product Team, Review Process, Quality Assurance, Decision Process, Documentation
  Governance, Version Strategy.
- **Later blocks:** added as the edition grows, always under the GoldBot Constitution.

## 4. Chapter Anatomy

Each chapter follows one shape: a header stating package, status, block, and **subordination**;
an Executive Summary; a chapter Table of Contents; numbered sections; and a closing marker.
Governance chapters cite the operative platform source they defer to.

## 5. Package Standard

Each chapter is delivered from a standardized work package (`01_TASK` … `08_CHANGELOG`), the
same standard the GoldBot Constitution uses, so every platform chapter is deterministic to
execute and review.

## 6. Extension versus Amendment

The Platform Constitution grows chiefly by **extension** (new chapters, new material) and is
**amended** only when settled platform meaning changes — a recorded, Product-Director-level act
that must remain consistent with the GoldBot Constitution and never weaken safety.

## 7. Single Source of Truth

Consistent with DR-016, the Platform Constitution states platform principles and **defers** to
single authoritative sources — the GoldBot Constitution for shared matters, and the operative
platform policies/standards for detail — rather than duplicating them.

## 8. Structural Integrity

The structure is kept sound by consistency: reserved terms carry one meaning (Chapter 07),
each chapter fits the anatomy above, nothing duplicates or contradicts the GoldBot Constitution
or an approved platform chapter, and subordination is stated in every chapter.
