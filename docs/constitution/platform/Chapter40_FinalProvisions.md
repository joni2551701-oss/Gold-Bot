## Executive Summary

Chapter 40 is the **final chapter** of the GoldBot Platform Constitution v1.0 edition. It states the
provisions that make the platform edition operative: how it is ratified, how it relates to the
GoldBot Constitution above it, how precedence and severability work, and the reaffirmation that the

## 1. Purpose of the Final Provisions

The final provisions bind the platform edition into an operative whole — how it takes effect, how it
sits beneath the GoldBot Constitution, and how it endures — the closing terms that turn forty
platform chapters into one instrument.

## 2. Ratification and Entry into Force

The Platform Constitution is adopted **by Director approval**. Each chapter enters the edition upon
its approval (Draft → Review → Approved → Completed), and Platform Version 1.0 is complete when the
full set of chapters, including this one, is approved. Entry into force follows the same governed
authorization as any consequential change.

## 3. Relationship to the GoldBot Constitution

The Platform Constitution is **subordinate** to the GoldBot Constitution v1.0 (DR-013), which is the
primary governing document. This edition governs the Platform layer beneath it. Where a matter is
Core, trading, or safety, the GoldBot Constitution governs; this edition states platform-scoped rules
and defers upward for the rest. Nothing here overrides the GoldBot Constitution.

## 4. Precedence and Consistency

Precedence is clear: the GoldBot Constitution first, then this Platform Constitution, then the
operative platform standards and policies. Within this edition the chapters are consistent by
construction — reserved terms carry one meaning (Chapter 07), each domain has one source of truth,
and no chapter contradicts an approved chapter, the GoldBot Constitution, or a Director ruling. An
inconsistency is resolved by amendment (Chapter 38), not silent reinterpretation.

## 5. Severability

If any single provision is found invalid, unworkable, or in need of change, it is amended or set
aside under the amendment process **without invalidating the rest** of the Platform Constitution.
A defect in one chapter does not bring down the whole; the remaining provisions continue in force.

## 6. Completeness of Version 1.0

Platform Version 1.0 comprises the Foundation block (01–07), the Governance block (08–17), the
Architecture block (18–27), the Domain block (28–37), and the Closing block (38–40). Upon approval
of this chapter, the platform edition is **complete** as a coherent whole; further growth proceeds by
extension and amendment (Chapters 38, 39), under the GoldBot Constitution.

## 7. Final Safety Affirmation

The Platform Constitution closes by reaffirming the inherited, non-amendable safety guarantees
(DR-015): across every surface, feature, plan, provider, and version, the **Risk Manager is never
bypassed**, surfaces **deliver only signals the Core has cleared** (Chapter 31), the **advisory
intelligence never executes**, and **human oversight is preserved**. No platform amendment, roadmap
item, or provision may weaken this. The user's structural protection is non-negotiable and
non-expiring.

## 8. Continuation

With Platform Version 1.0 complete, the Platform layer continues under both constitutions: surfaces
grow on the shared model, over the gateway, on top of a stable Core, and this edition is extended
routinely and amended rarely — always beneath the GoldBot Constitution.
