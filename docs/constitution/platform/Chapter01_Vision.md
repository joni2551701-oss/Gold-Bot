## Executive Summary

Chapter 01 states the **vision for the GoldBot Platform layer** — the family of client
surfaces (Telegram, Web, Mobile, and future clients) through which users reach GoldBot.
The vision is a single, platform-neutral surface layer: one shared model expressed across
many platforms, each reaching the Core only through the gateway, none holding trading
logic. The Platform layer is where GoldBot meets its users; this chapter fixes what that
layer aspires to be.

## 1. Vision Statement

The GoldBot Platform layer exists to present GoldBot's capabilities to users through **one
shared, platform-neutral model**, expressed consistently across every client and reaching
the Core only through the gateway. Platforms are thin, governed surfaces — they present and
collect, they never decide.

## 2. Long-Term Vision (5–10 Years)

Over the long horizon, the Platform layer is intended to grow **by adding surfaces on one
model**, not by fragmenting into many bespoke clients. New platforms are added at the
perimeter without changing the Core (GoldBot Constitution, Platform Architecture chapter),
and each speaks the same shared model. A decade out, a capability defined once should still
be expressed consistently across every surface then in use.

## 3. Purpose of the Platform Layer

The purpose of the Platform layer is to **deliver GoldBot's intelligence to users** —
clearly, consistently, and safely — while keeping all logic and authority in the Core.
Platforms translate the shared model into each client's real presentation; they carry no
trading decision and no route around the Core's protections.

## 4. Strategic Objectives

1. **One platform model.** A single, platform-neutral model expressed across all surfaces.
2. **Gateway-only access.** Every platform reaches the Core solely through the gateway.
3. **No trading logic in platforms.** Presentation and intent live at the perimeter;
   decisions live in the Core.
4. **Honest capability declaration.** Each surface states what it supports and, where it
   does not, why.
5. **Core platform independence preserved.** Platforms adapt to the Core; they never push
   platform assumptions inward.
6. **Consistency across surfaces.** The same capability behaves recognizably everywhere.

## 5. Success Definition

The Platform layer succeeds when: every surface reaches the Core only through the gateway;
no platform holds trading logic or bypasses the risk controls; capabilities are expressed
consistently across clients; new platforms have been added on the shared model without Core
change; and the user's experience is coherent from one surface to the next.

## 6. Non-Goals

- **Not a decision-maker.** Platforms do not decide trades or hold trading logic.
- **Not a second entry point.** No platform reaches the Core outside the gateway.
- **Not a place for Core logic.** Business and trading logic never migrate into a surface.
- **Not a fragmented set of bespoke clients.** Divergent, per-platform models are a non-goal.

## 7. Platform Philosophy

- **Thin surfaces, strong Core.** Value and authority stay in the Core; platforms stay thin.
- **One model, many expressions.** Define once, present everywhere.
- **Boundaries are features.** Gateway-only access and no-trading-logic are the layer's most
  valuable properties.
- **Honesty about capability.** A surface states its true state, including its limits.

## 8. Future-Proof Principles

1. **One model, evolving surfaces.** The shared model is stable; individual clients evolve.
2. **Gateway-only, forever.** However many surfaces appear, they enter through the gateway.
3. **Core independence preserved.** The Core never learns platform specifics.
4. **Additive growth.** New platforms are additions, not rewrites.
5. **Safety is inherited and never weakened.** The Platform layer operates under the GoldBot
   Constitution's non-amendable safety guarantees (DR-015) and may never bypass them.
