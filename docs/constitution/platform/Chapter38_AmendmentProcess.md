## Executive Summary

Chapter 38 opens the Platform closing block by stating the **amendment process for the Platform
Constitution** — how this edition is changed. It mirrors the GoldBot Constitution's amendment
discipline (extend routinely, amend rarely) and adds one binding constraint: no platform amendment
may ever conflict with, or weaken anything in, the GoldBot Constitution above it.

## 1. Amendment Statement

An amendment is a **deliberate change to an approved part of the Platform Constitution**. The
process makes such change orderly, authorized, and recorded, so the platform edition can adapt
without losing stability — always beneath the GoldBot Constitution.

## 2. Extension versus Amendment

The Platform Constitution grows chiefly by **extension** — new chapters or material that does not
change settled meaning — and is **amended** only when approved platform meaning changes. Most
growth is extension; amendment is exceptional (Chapter 10).

## 3. What Requires an Amendment

An amendment is required to alter an approved platform chapter's stated principle or to move a
platform boundary this edition defines. Routine additions and clarifications that do not change
meaning are extensions. A change that alters settled platform meaning is treated as an amendment.

## 4. Authority to Amend

Platform amendment is a **Product-Director-level act**, and where it touches Core or safety it
requires the **GoldBot Director** (Chapter 11). No amendment takes effect without the appropriate
authorization, and none may be made unilaterally by execution.

## 5. Amendment Procedure

A platform amendment follows the governed discipline: proposed with rationale, reviewed for
consistency with the rest of the Platform Constitution **and** the GoldBot Constitution, authorized,
and only then adopted. An amendment inconsistent with either constitution is reconciled before
adoption.

## 6. Recording Amendments

Every platform amendment is **recorded** with its rationale and authorization, so the platform
edition's history stays traceable — what changed, when, and why (Chapter 15).

## 7. Amendments and the GoldBot Constitution

No platform amendment may **conflict with or weaken** the GoldBot Constitution. Where the GoldBot
Constitution governs a matter, this edition defers to it; a platform amendment can refine
platform-scoped rules but never override the primary document (DR-013).

## 8. Amendments and Safety

No platform amendment may weaken Trading Safety. The guarantees that the Risk Manager is never
bypassed and that the advisory intelligence never executes — including that surfaces deliver only
cleared signals (Chapter 31) — are non-amendable in this edition (DR-015). An amendment that would
breach them is out of bounds regardless of authorization.
