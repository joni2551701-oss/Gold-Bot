## Executive Summary

Chapter 11 defines the **Product Director** — the role that sets direction for the Platform
layer, reviews surface work, and authorizes platform change. The Product Director's authority
is real but **derived**: it operates under the GoldBot Director and the GoldBot Constitution,
and it cannot touch Core governance or the safety guarantees.

## 1. Role Statement

The Product Director **sets direction for the Platform layer and authorizes platform change**.
The role decides which surfaces and platform capabilities are built and in what order, and
grants the authorization for a platform change to take effect — all under the GoldBot
Constitution.

## 2. Authority

The Product Director may: define and prioritize platform work; approve or reject surface
designs and deliverables; authorize platform merges; and rule on platform questions escalated
by the Product Team. This authority is the mechanism by which platform direction enters the
system, within the bounds of Section 6.

## 3. Responsibilities

The Product Director keeps platform direction consistent with the platform vision, mission,
and principles and with the GoldBot Constitution; reviews surface work against the standards;
rules on escalations; and ensures consequential platform decisions are recorded.

## 4. Review and Approval

No consequential platform change takes effect without the Product Director's review and
authorization. Designs are reviewed before implementation and deliverables before merge,
applying the platform review checklist and the inherited safety gate. Approval is explicit and
recorded.

## 5. Direction and Prioritization

The Product Director sets and re-orders the platform roadmap — which surfaces and capabilities
proceed and when — within the platform scope and non-goals, and never toward a direction the
GoldBot Constitution forbids.

## 6. Limits of Authority

The Product Director's authority is **bounded and subordinate**. It cannot override the GoldBot
Director or the GoldBot Constitution; it cannot introduce a second entry point to the Core,
place trading logic in a surface, or weaken a safety guarantee (DR-015). Matters touching Core
or safety are escalated to the GoldBot Director.

## 7. Interaction with the Product Team

The Product Director issues platform direction; the Product Team executes it (Chapter 12). The
Product Director answers the team's escalations and reviews its deliverables. The roles are
deliberately separate.

## 8. Accountability

The Product Director is accountable for keeping platform authority within the GoldBot
Constitution and for the integrity of platform decisions. Because direction and approvals are
recorded, the role's choices remain part of the audit trail.
