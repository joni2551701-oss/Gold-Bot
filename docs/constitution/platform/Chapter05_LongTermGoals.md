## Executive Summary

Chapter 05 states the **long-term goals of the Platform layer** — where the layer intends to
be across a five-to-ten-year horizon, expressed in terms of reach, consistency, and
governance rather than any single feature. Every goal is an application of the platform
principles over a longer timeframe, and none weakens the Core's guarantees.

## 1. Five-Year Goals

Within roughly five years, the Platform layer aims to: operate **multiple mature surfaces**
(the Telegram bot alongside Web and Mobile clients) on one shared model; reach the Core in
all of them **only through the gateway**; and deliver every new surface as a governed
addition, with no trading logic having entered any platform.

## 2. Ten-Year Goals

Within roughly ten years, the Platform layer aims to: support a **broad family of surfaces**
on the same shared model; preserve the gateway-only and no-trading-logic boundaries across
the entire history; and accommodate new client types entirely at the perimeter, without
Core change.

## 3. Multi-Platform Maturity

The layer aims for **maturity across platforms** — Telegram, Web, Mobile, and future clients
each expressing the shared model well in their own idiom, with consistent capability and
honest declaration. Maturity is measured by consistency and safety, not by count of features.

## 4. Growth Model

Growth follows one model: a new surface is added as a governed client that adapts the shared
model and reaches the Core through the gateway, passing design and documentation before code
and review before merge. The shared model is not reshaped to fit a surface; surfaces adapt to
the model.

## 5. Consistency Goals

The layer aims to keep **one coherent experience** as surfaces multiply: a capability defined
once behaves recognizably everywhere, and users move between clients without relearning.
Consistency is a standing goal, protected as the surface count grows.

## 6. Accessibility Goals

The layer aims for each surface to meet its platform's **accessibility expectations**, so
GoldBot's capabilities are usable broadly. Accessibility is pursued per surface, within the
shared model.

## 7. Safety Goals

Across every goal and every horizon, the Platform layer preserves the Core's protections: the
risk controls are never bypassed and the advisory intelligence never executes, on any surface
(DR-015). Safety is a constant of the goals, not a variable.

## 8. Success Indicators

Progress is indicated by whether, over time: every surface reached the Core only through the
gateway; no platform held trading logic or bypassed the risk controls; capabilities stayed
consistent across clients; new surfaces were added on the shared model without Core change;
and the safety guarantees remained intact throughout.
