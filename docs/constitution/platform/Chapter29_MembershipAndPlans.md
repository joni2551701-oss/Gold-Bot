## Executive Summary

Chapter 29 describes **membership and plans** — how the Platform layer structures a user's
standing and the bundles of access available to them. Membership and plans build on the
subscription system (Chapter 21): they define **what access a user has**, never how trading works.
This chapter states the model and its boundary.

## 1. Membership Statement

**Membership** is a user's standing on the platform; a **plan** is a defined bundle of access a
member holds. Together they organize what features and Core capabilities a user may reach — an
access concern, not a trading concern.

## 2. Members and Standing

A member is a user with a recognized standing (for example free, subscribed, or a defined tier).
Standing determines the plan a user holds and, through it, their entitlements (Chapter 21).

## 3. Plans and Bundled Access

A plan bundles **entitlements** into a coherent offering. Plans change what a member can access
and how surfaces present features to them; they never change the Core's logic, the risk controls,
or the safety guarantees.

## 4. Enforcement Through Entitlements

Plans are enforced through entitlements and authorization at the gateway (Chapters 20, 21). A
member reaches a gated capability only when their plan's entitlements permit it; membership
creates no separate access path and no bypass.

## 5. Plan Lifecycle

A plan and a membership have a governed lifecycle — joined, active, upgraded or downgraded,
lapsed — with an auditable record. Transitions are orderly and recorded, so a member's access
reflects their current plan.

## 6. The Membership Boundary

Membership and plans govern **access only**. No membership standing, plan, or transition may
bypass the Risk Manager, place trading logic in a surface, or grant the advisory intelligence a
route to act (DR-015). A richer plan grants more access, never different trading behavior or
weaker safety.

## 7. Fairness and Transparency

Plans and their entitlements are presented **transparently**, so members understand what a plan
grants. Honest capability declaration applies (Chapter 04): what a plan includes and excludes is
clear.

## 8. Evolution

Membership and plans evolve by adding standings, plans, and lifecycle capability behind stable
platform contracts, without changing the Core or weakening a protection.
