## Executive Summary

Chapter 03 states the **values of the Platform layer** — the convictions that decide how the
mission is pursued when a choice arises. Platform values place consistency, safety, and Core
integrity above surface-specific convenience or speed. This chapter states those values
across the platform's work.

## 1. Values Statement

The Platform layer values a **consistent, safe, Core-faithful experience** above the
convenience of any single surface. When two acceptable choices compete, the values decide.

## 2. Consistency

A capability defined once is expressed **recognizably everywhere**. Consistency across
surfaces is valued over per-platform cleverness, because a coherent experience is what makes
the ecosystem trustworthy to users.

## 3. Platform Neutrality

The shared model is **platform-neutral**, and that neutrality is valued: each surface adapts
the model to its own presentation without pushing its assumptions back into the model or the
Core. Neutrality is what lets new platforms join without disruption.

## 4. Thin Surfaces

Platforms are valued for being **thin**: they present and collect, and they keep logic and
authority in the Core. A surface that accumulates its own logic is drifting away from this
value and toward risk.

## 5. Honesty of Capability

A surface **declares its true capabilities**, including where it does not support something
and why. Honest declaration is valued over the appearance of completeness, because the true
state of the ecosystem must always be visible.

## 6. User Experience Values

- **Clarity.** What the user sees is clear and faithful to the Core.
- **Control.** The user remains in control of consequential action.
- **Coherence.** The experience holds together across surfaces.
- **Trustworthiness.** What is shown has a governed origin.

## 7. Safety Values

The Platform layer values the **Core's protections as inviolable**. No surface value —
speed, richness, convenience — is ever placed above the non-amendable safety guarantees
(DR-015): the risk controls are never bypassed and the advisory intelligence never executes,
on any surface.

## 8. Value Compliance

Compliance is demonstrated: a surface choice that would add trading logic, bypass the
gateway, or weaken a safety guarantee is rejected regardless of its appeal; consistency and
honest capability declaration are treated as review criteria; and where two options are both
acceptable, the record shows the values were used to choose.
