## Executive Summary

Chapter 39 states the **roadmap and evolution model for the Platform layer** — where the platform
goes and the single rule by which it grows. The platform evolves by adding governed surfaces and
capabilities on the shared model, over the gateway, on top of the completed Core. This chapter
states the baseline, the forward work, and the evolution principles that keep growth safe.

## 1. Roadmap Statement

The platform roadmap describes the Platform layer's **planned evolution** — the ordered work by
which surfaces and platform capabilities grow on top of the completed Core, through the gateway. It
is a plan for addition, not for reshaping the Core.

## 2. The Baseline

The roadmap begins from the **Core-complete baseline** (GoldBot Constitution, Roadmap chapter) and
the **platform foundation** — the shared model, registry, capability model, and the existing
Telegram surface. From here, platform work is added on top of the gateway.

## 3. Platform Work Ahead

The forward platform work includes maturing the existing surface and adding new ones — Web and
Mobile clients on the shared model — and deepening platform capabilities (user management,
memberships, notifications, settings, analytics) as governed work packages, each consuming the Core
only through the gateway.

## 4. The Growth Model

Growth follows one model: a new surface or capability enters as a governed work package that adapts
the shared model and reaches the Core through the gateway, passing design and documentation before
code and review before merge. The shared model is not reshaped to fit a surface; surfaces adapt to
it.

## 5. Milestones

The platform roadmap advances by milestones, each a set of governed work packages with their own
reviews and acceptance: a matured primary surface; then additional surfaces on the shared model;
then deeper platform capabilities. No milestone is reached by weakening a boundary.

## 6. Evolution Principles

Platform evolution obeys the platform future-proof principles (Chapter 01): one model with evolving
surfaces; gateway-only access forever; Core independence preserved; versioned compatibility; and
amend rarely, extend routinely. These are the tests every roadmap item passes.

## 7. Roadmap and Safety

No platform roadmap item may weaken Trading Safety. Every future surface and capability preserves
the guarantees that the Risk Manager is never bypassed, that only cleared signals are delivered
(Chapter 31), and that the advisory intelligence never executes (DR-015). Safety is a constant of
the roadmap.

## 8. The Living Roadmap

The platform roadmap is a living plan: the Product Director sets and re-orders it (Chapter 11),
under the GoldBot Director and Constitution, and it grows as work packages are added and completed,
each closing with recorded lessons and cross-project notes. Direction may adjust; the growth model
and safety guarantees do not.
