# DD-031 — MemoryReader — Full Platform Interface

**Type**: Director Decision Record (Director-issued, binding).
**Governance**: v1.1. **Phase**: GoldBot v1.1 Phase 1 — Market Data Foundation.
**Context**: MemoryReader was specified as a minimal read API.

## Decision

`MemoryReader` is the **full** platform interface, not just reads:

    get_last_candle()  get_series()  get_last_closed()  get_forming()
    subscribe()  unsubscribe()  health()  snapshot()

Every future platform works through exactly this interface.

## Why

A single, complete contract is what makes every client (Telegram, Mini
App, Android, iOS, Desktop, Web, AI, Chart) platform-independent and
swappable over the same Memory.

## Status

**Approved — binding for the Market Data Foundation.** Reflected in the
architecture document (§7 (MemoryReader contract)).

## Applies to

The GoldBot v1.1 Market Data Foundation (`data/` layer) and every future
component that reads market data through it.
