# DD-037 — Market Data Foundation Architecture Freeze

**Type**: Director Decision Record (Director-issued, binding).
**Governance**: v1.1.
**Context**: The GoldBot v1.1 Phase 1 architecture
(`docs/architecture/MARKET_DATA_FOUNDATION.md`) was reviewed and approved as
the official architecture, incorporating amendments DD-026…DD-035.

## Decision

Upon approval, the **Market Data Foundation architecture is FROZEN.**
During the implementation phase, **significant architectural changes may be
introduced only through a new Director Decision** — implementation must
follow the frozen design, not silently reshape it.

Minor, non-architectural implementation detail (naming within the agreed
modules, private helpers, test structure) does not require a new decision;
any change to the module topology, the contracts (MemoryReader, events,
snapshot), the multi-asset registry, or the flows requires Director
approval.

## Why

Freezing the design at approval prevents architectural drift during coding —
the same discipline that produced GoldBot's governance. It guarantees the
implementation delivers the reviewed architecture, and that any material
change is a deliberate, recorded decision rather than an in-flight
improvisation.

## Status

**Approved — the architecture document is FROZEN** as of its approval.

## Applies to

`docs/architecture/MARKET_DATA_FOUNDATION.md` and the v1.1 Phase 1
implementation that follows it.
