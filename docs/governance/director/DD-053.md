# DD-053 — Module Acceptance Registry (MA-xxx)

**Type**: Director Decision Record (Director-issued, binding).
**Governance**: v1.1. Process rule.
**Context**: Module *acceptances* were initially recorded as Director
Decisions (DD-040, DD-041), and a PR-#7 review label ("DD-042 Batch
Record") collided with the already-assigned DD-042 (Monotonic Sequence
Guarantee). Reusing DD numbers for acceptances risks overwriting real
architecture/policy records as the module count grows.

## Decision

**Module acceptances are no longer Director Decisions.** They are recorded
in a separate registry under the identifier scheme:

```
MA-001   MarketMemory Core
MA-002   MemoryReader
MA-003   Candle Builder
MA-004   Price Stream
...      (MA = Module Acceptance)
```

Consequences:
- **DD-xxx** numbers are reserved for **architecture and governance
  decisions** only.
- **MA-xxx** records hold **module acceptances**, with their own audit
  history under `docs/governance/module_acceptance/`.
- This keeps numbering unambiguous even at 50+ modules.

The earlier acceptance decisions **DD-040** (MarketMemory Core) and
**DD-041** (MemoryReader) are superseded *as identifiers* by **MA-001**
and **MA-002** respectively; their content is preserved in the MA
registry. No architecture/policy DD is renumbered.

## Why

Separating "we decided X about the architecture" from "module N passed
review" gives each a clean, non-colliding audit trail, and prevents the
DD stream from being diluted by routine per-module acceptances.

## Status

**Approved — standing rule.** Future acceptances use MA-xxx; DD-xxx is
architecture/governance only.

## Applies to

Every module acceptance from this decision onward, and the
`docs/governance/module_acceptance/` registry.
