# DD-039 — Market Data Foundation is the Canonical Architecture

**Type**: Director Decision Record (Director-issued, binding).
**Governance**: v1.1.
**Context**: After PR #3 passed the CI gate and Final Director Review, the
Market Data Foundation architecture was formally accepted and merged to
`main` (merge commit of PR #3).

## Decision

`docs/architecture/MARKET_DATA_FOUNDATION.md` is the **Canonical
Architecture** for the GoldBot v1.1 Phase 1 implementation. The
implementation **must not deviate** from it; any significant change to the
architecture is permitted **only through a new Director Decision** (this
reinforces the freeze established in DD-037).

## Why

A single canonical architecture, merged and frozen, is the reference every
Phase 1 module is measured against. It removes ambiguity about "the design,"
prevents in-flight redesign, and makes any deviation a visible, deliberate,
recorded decision rather than silent drift.

## Status

**Approved — the Market Data Foundation is the canonical, frozen architecture
for v1.1 Phase 1.** Merged to `main` via PR #3.

## Applies to

The entire GoldBot v1.1 Phase 1 implementation and every module realized
under it.
