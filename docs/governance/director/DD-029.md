# DD-029 — Memory Versioning (per-timeframe revision)

**Type**: Director Decision Record (Director-issued, binding).
**Governance**: v1.1. **Phase**: GoldBot v1.1 Phase 1 — Market Data Foundation.
**Context**: Consumers needed a cheap way to detect new data.

## Decision

Each timeframe carries a **`revision`** number. Every update does
`revision++`. Chart and AI detect new data by comparing revisions rather
than re-scanning contents.

## Why

A monotonic revision is an O(1) change signal — charts/AI refresh only
when data actually changed, complementing the event bus (DD-028).

## Status

**Approved — binding for the Market Data Foundation.** Reflected in the
architecture document (§5, §7 (versioning)).

## Applies to

The GoldBot v1.1 Market Data Foundation (`data/` layer) and every future
component that reads market data through it.
