# DD-004 — Governance Records

**Type**: Director Decision Record (Director-issued, binding).
**Governance**: v1.1 (FROZEN).
**Context**: The Worker flagged (No Silent Decisions) that Director
Decisions №1–№3 lived only in session/chat history and were not yet
persisted to a controlled document. The Director ratifies persisting
them and establishes a standing policy for all future decisions.

## Decision

**1. Persist the decision set.** Director Decisions №1–№4 are recorded
as controlled Decision Records under:

```
docs/governance/director/
├── README.md      (registry)
├── DD-001.md
├── DD-002.md
├── DD-003.md
└── DD-004.md      (this record)
```

The **versioned** layout (individual `DD-XXX.md` identifiers) was chosen
over a single `DIRECTOR_DECISIONS.md` file, because the number of
Director decisions grows over time and individual identifiers are
easier to manage and cross-reference.

**2. Standing Director Policy (in force from this record onward).**

> No architecture-, governance-, or repository-level Director decision
> may remain only in chat history. Once ratified, it is preserved in the
> governance documents as a formal Decision Record.

This preserves decision consistency and an audit trail as the project
grows.

## Execution Constraint

This record and its siblings are documented on the **working branch**
(`claude/trading-ai-arch-review-tgszrz`), **not** on `main`, until
Recovery completes. Recording a Director decision:

- does **not** interfere with the Recovery Git sequence;
- does **not** change the production branch state;
- does **not** conflict with the Migration SSOT
  (`docs/governance/MIGRATION_PLAN.md`).

Governance recording is a documentation act, deliberately separated
from the Recovery Git operations, which restore repository state.

## Why

Decisions kept only in chat are not auditable and can be lost or
misremembered as the project evolves. A controlled, versioned registry
gives every ratified Director ruling a durable identifier and a stable
location, independent of conversation history — which is exactly the
kind of traceability GoldBot's governance is built to guarantee.

## Status

**Approved — a standing rule for all future Director decisions.**

## Applies to

Every future architecture-, governance-, or repository-level Director
decision; the `docs/governance/director/` registry that holds them.
