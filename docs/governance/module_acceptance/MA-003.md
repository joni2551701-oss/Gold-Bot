## Scope

`data_layer/live_data/candle_clock.py` (pure, clock-independent timeframe-boundary math,
generalizes `data_cache._get_next_candle_time`, Art. 11) and
`data_layer/live_data/candle_builder.py` (single-writer OHLC aggregation into
MarketMemory). Additive, backward-compatible Module 1 extensions:
`CandleSource.REPLAY` (DD-043) and `TimeframeMemory.open_candle(when=…)`
(DD-044).

## Basis for acceptance

- Amendments DD-042 (monotonic + replay-identical sequence), DD-043
  (canonical source values), DD-044 (clock independence), DD-045 (M1→D1
  ordering) all satisfied.
- Single-writer, deterministic, replay-compatible, immutable closed
  candles, atomic new-candle, event-hook-ready (module 7), no business
  logic.
- Isolated from the pipeline. 37 tests incl. concurrency + replay
  determinism; full suite green, no regression.
